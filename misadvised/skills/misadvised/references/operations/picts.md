### PICTS

**Pictorial-Illustration Conceptual-Typography Sometimes-Sequential**

PICTS is an operation of rendering a word with large letterforms as a set of internally coherent and holistically related editorial illustrations with background default of transparent PNG. The relationship among the illustrations may be historically sequential, thematic, conceptual, or symbolic but must otherwise adhere to the user's input.

PICTS is a form of conceptual typographic illustration using pictorial lettering, sometimes sequentially, always faithful to the input request. Each letter is usually uppercase. Each letter functions as a crisp, enclosed frame containing a self-contained pictorial composition. The letters are bold and heavily outlined, with solid, clean edges and no drop shadow, glow, or other soft edge treatment. The default frame color is gold, but other colors can be requested.

PICTS letterforms function as pictorial containers. The harness's ordinary text color and solid-fill rules therefore do not govern their illustrated interiors or frame colors. The MISADVISED palette and visual language apply throughout.

All colors and each individual letter's enclosed generated depiction follow the MISADVISED style harness. The individual scenes do NOT bleed outside of the frame of the letter but are bounded like a clipping mask.

Together, the illustrated letters, each composition intelligible in its own right, form a word whose constituent imagery collectively articulates its meaning, subject, or narrative. The full word is rendered as a PNG with a transparent background.
